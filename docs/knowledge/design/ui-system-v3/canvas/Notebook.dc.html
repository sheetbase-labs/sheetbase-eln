Objective

Screen three depth-filter trains for clarification of AAV9 harvest lot HL-0934 and select one for the 50 L tech-transfer run. Acceptance: capsid step yield ≥ 80 % with filtrate turbidity ≤ 20 NTU.

Method

Harvest was held at 2–8 °C and filtered at a constant flux of 100 LMH. Filtrate was sampled at 50, 100 and 150 L/m² for turbidity and capsid titre (ELISA, method EM-004). Feed titre 8.6 × 10¹¹ cp/mL.
