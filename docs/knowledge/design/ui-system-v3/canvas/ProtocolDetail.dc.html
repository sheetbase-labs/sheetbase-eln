Protocols / PRT-021

Depth-filter clarification screen

Version 3 ▾Export PDFNew experiment from this
Effective since 2 Sep 2026Owner Gareth P.AAV · downstreamPeriodic review due 2 Sep 2027

1 · Purpose

Screen depth-filter trains for clarification of AAV harvest at constant flux and select a train and maximum load for scale-up.

2 · Parameters

ParameterValueUnitTolerance
Flux100LMH± 5
Load points50, 100, 150L/m²—
Harvest hold2–8°C≤ 14 days
Pressure loggingevery 10L/m²—

3 · Procedure

  1. Equilibrate each filter train with 50 L/m² PBS. Record the flush volume.
  2. Load harvest at constant flux. Log pressure every 10 L/m².
  3. Collect filtrate at each load point into labelled tubes (register each as a sample).
  4. Measure turbidity on TB-02 and capsid titre by ELISA (method EM-004).
  5. Calculate step yield against the feed titre.

4 · Acceptance criteria

Capsid step yield ≥ 80 % and filtrate turbidity ≤ 20 NTU at the selected load.
