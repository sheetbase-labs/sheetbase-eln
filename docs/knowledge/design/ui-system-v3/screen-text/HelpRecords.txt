Signatures
A signature is your password entered again at the moment of signing, plus a meaning: Authored, Reviewed, Approved or Witnessed. It is bound to one version of the record by its content hash, so any later change needs an amendment and a new signature.
What reviewers see
Reviewers see changes since their last review marked in the margin, can comment on any block or cell, and either sign or return the record with comments. They can’t edit the author’s content.
