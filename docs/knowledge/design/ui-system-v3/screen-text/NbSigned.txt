Objective
Choose the harvest time for AAV9 production in HEK293 suspension culture: highest genome titre while keeping viability at harvest at or above 80 %.
Method
Triplicate 125 mL shake flasks per condition, transfected at 2.0 × 10⁶ cells/mL with a 2:1 PEI:DNA ratio. Harvested at 48, 72 and 96 h. Genome titre by ddPCR ( DS-0296 ), viability by ViCell ( DS-0297 ).

Conclusion
Harvest at 72 h. 96 h added about 4 % titre but viability fell to 61.8 %, which increases host-cell impurities for downstream. 72 h is carried into EXP-0138 and EXP-0142.
