Depth-filter clarification screen

1 · Purpose
Screen depth-filter trains for clarification of AAV harvest at constant flux and select a train and maximum load for scale-up.
2 · Parameters
| | Parameter | Value | Unit | Tolerance
| Flux | 100 | LMH | ± 5
| Load points | 50, 100, 150 | L/m² | —
| Harvest hold | 2–8 | °C | ≤ 14 days
| Pressure logging | every 10 | L/m² | —
3 · Procedure
Equilibrate each filter train with 50 L/m² PBS. Record the flush volume.
Load harvest at constant flux. Log pressure every 10 L/m².
Collect filtrate at each load point into labelled tubes (register each as a sample).
Measure turbidity on TB-02 and capsid titre by ELISA (method EM-004).
Calculate step yield against the feed titre.
4 · Acceptance criteria
Capsid step yield ≥ 80 % and filtrate turbidity ≤ 20 NTU at the selected load.
