Objective
Find the PEI:DNA ratio (w/w) that maximises AAV9 genome titre in HEK293 suspension culture at 1.0 µg DNA per mL, without viability at harvest falling below 80 %.
Method
Cells were seeded at 2.0 × 10⁶ cells/mL in 125 mL shake flasks (30 mL working volume) and transfected with the triple-plasmid mix at 1.0 µg DNA/mL (0.5 pg DNA per cell) . PEI:DNA ratios of 1:1, 1.5:1, 2:1 and 3:1 (w/w) were run in duplicate. Harvest at 72 h; titre by qPCR genome titre by ddPCR ( DS-0302 ) , viability by ViCell ( DS-0303 ).

Conclusion
A 2:1 PEI:DNA ratio gave the highest genome titre (7.2 × 10¹⁰ vg/mL) with viability above 80 %. 3:1 reduced viability to 72.6 % with no titre gain. Recommend 2:1 for the 50 L engineering run.
