# What happened to every audit issue

Every issue in [`ISSUES.md`](ISSUES.md) was carried by one of eighteen file-disjoint fix slices and
is accounted for below. An issue that one slice could not reach was handed to the slice that owns
the file; where both recorded it, the outcome shown is the owner's.

| Issues | **91** |
| fixed | 82 |
| copy-corrected | 3 |
| not-a-defect | 3 |
| blocked | 3 |

`fixed` changed behaviour; `copy-corrected` changed only wording that was wrong; `not-a-defect` is
recorded with the evidence that disproves it (two are artifacts of the audit harness's own dev-only
overlay, one is legal markup); `blocked` needs a product decision and is listed at the end.
