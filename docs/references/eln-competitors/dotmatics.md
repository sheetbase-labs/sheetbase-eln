# Dotmatics — ELN / Scientific Platform Pattern Reference

**Research snapshot:** 13 August 2026  
**Primary relevance to Sheetbase:** structured-at-capture data, flexible canvases/templates, Excel-like tables, workflow automation and agentic configuration.

## Product pattern

Dotmatics increasingly positions its ELN as a structured scientific-data foundation rather than only an electronic notebook.

Its current public material emphasises a configurable scientific canvas, structured fields/templates, native scientific data types, search/dashboards, integrations, automation and AI operating on structured scientific records.

## Structured capture and tables

Dotmatics describes Canvas as able to hold tables, chemical structures, images, sketches, spectra and other scientific information. Current material advertises single-click cell editing, Excel-like keyboard navigation and preserved column settings alongside structured records.

### Sheetbase lesson

Excel-like interaction is compatible with a structured scientific platform. Familiarity can be the capture layer while durable semantics remain underneath.

## Templates and data models

Dotmatics advertises flexible protocol/experiment templates ranging from structured forms to looser capture. Its Luma material also discusses configuration of workflows, tasks, data models and governance settings.

### Sheetbase lesson

Model, Pipeline and configuration architecture should be designed coherently even if delivered sequentially.

## Workflow and automation

Dotmatics assay-data products support manual and automated processing and configurable scientific protocols. Luma is positioned as a low/no-code adaptive workflow layer.

## Agentic direction

Current Luma/Luma Agent positioning is strategically relevant: natural-language analysis, reporting, workflow management and platform configuration over structured scientific data, with logged/reviewable actions in published material.

### Sheetbase lesson

Do not bolt AI onto an unstructured Notebook. Make Models, Datasets and versioned Pipeline definitions the substrate agents operate against.

## Permissions and regulated use

Dotmatics markets granular permissions, role-based access, audit trails and electronic signatures. The architectural lesson is that workflow/data configuration and scientific content require explicit governance.

## What Sheetbase should copy conceptually

- Excel-like capture without abandoning structured Records;
- flexible canvases containing native structured scientific objects;
- structured-at-capture data as the basis of analytics/AI;
- configurable workflows/data models;
- AI as an author/executor of inspectable plans rather than only a summariser;
- strong integration/API mindset.

## What Sheetbase can improve on

Keep the low-level primitives explicit. Model, Dataset, Sheet, Notebook and Pipeline should remain understandable through one object/API system rather than appearing as loosely related platform products.
